# 1.1.0

Requires macOS 27+. Build 9.

### Two-Scene Story Creator
- Introduces `TwoSceneStoryCreator` ("2-Scene Story Creator: Witty Curiosity → Full News").
- Scene 1 hooks the audience with witty curiosity; Scene 2 delivers the full verified news story with satisfying closure.
- Interconnected dialogue flow where each exchange develops logically with clear cause-and-effect beats.
- Dynamic cast of characters tailored to the subject matter rather than a rigid two-speaker limit.
- Completely positive prompt instructions guiding tone, pacing (25–35 words per scene), and character depth.
- 4 dedicated workflow presets in the Pipeline tab:
  - **Fast 2-Scene Viral Story**: Studio News → News Researcher → Grounded Joke Generator → 2-Scene Story Creator → Script Chat.
  - **Direct News to 2-Scene Script**: Studio News → News Researcher → 2-Scene Story Creator.
  - **Quick Joke to 2-Scene Story**: Grounded Joke Generator → 2-Scene Story Creator.
  - **2-Scene Story & ScriptChat Polish**: 2-Scene Story Creator → Script Chat.
- Seamless "Save to Library" integration with formatted 2-scene script exports.

### Discovery & Regional Preferences
- NewsSearch 0.2.0 and TrendingHashtag 0.2.0 power Studio and Pipeline discovery.
- App-wide Region Geo configuration in Settings, dynamically applied to Google Trends, RSS feeds, and News Researcher.
- Stable Studio local feeds with isolated refresh controls.

### Pipeline Execution & ScriptChat
- Step-by-step and continuous execution modes with live stage progress and stop control.
- ScriptChat dialogue refinement and script polishing with clear, unclipped output presentation.

### Native Library & Publishing
- Native AVPlayerView video previews preventing initialisation aborts on macOS 27.
- One-click Telegram bot and Google Drive / Google Sheets uploads via saved Keychain credentials.
- Clipboard copy actions for story images and generated assets.

Includes all capabilities and fixes prepared in 1.0.0 and 1.0.1.
